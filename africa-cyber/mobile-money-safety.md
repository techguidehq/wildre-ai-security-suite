# Mobile-Money Safety Checklist

Educational only; not affiliated with a provider.
- Be wary of urgency, threats, unexpected rewards, or requests to reverse a transaction.
- Never share PINs, passwords, OTPs, or recovery codes.
- Do not install apps or open links just because a message claims your account is blocked.
- Verify through the official app or independently obtained official contact information.
- Do not trust contact details included in a suspicious message.

If concerned: stop interacting; preserve screenshots safely; contact the provider via independently verified channels; ask how to secure the account/dispute a transaction; report through official channels. Never publish account identifiers or unredacted statements.
